# Story Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Export → Story map…: one self-contained, dark, script-free HTML page that shows a non-writer everything written so far in a screenplay or stage play (its shape, its people, every scene in order with a line or two from the page, and the gaps), readable at a glance on a phone.

**Architecture:** A new DOM-free package, `@drafter/story-map`, turns what Drafter already computes (the parse, the pages, the scene index, the cast, the Setup and Payoff notes) into one plain **model** (`buildStoryMap`), then draws the model as an HTML string (`renderStoryMap`) with hand-built SVG, three embedded WOFF2 fonts, and the HTML export's CSP without a script. The web app builds it in the export worker after a short **Make a story map** dialog; the CLI gains `drafter build --story-map`. Choices about the text live in the text (`[[Quote]]`, `[[No quote]]`, `[[Interval]]`, `[[No interval]]`, `Logline:`), page-wide ones in the metadata block (`storyMap.hooks`, `storyMap.plannedPages`), the rest on the project in this browser.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest + fast-check + jsdom, Playwright + axe, Vue 3.5 / Nuxt 4 (the dialog), Electron (menus), fontTools `pyftsubset` and `varLib.instancer` (a one-off, not a dependency).

**Spec:** `docs/drafter-story-map-design.md` (approved 2026-11-01; its 28 Calls bind). The approved mockups, local and never committed: `index.html` (a screenplay in progress), `finished.html`, and `stage.html` (a play), in the session's `glance-mockup/` folder. Read their markup for structure and class vocabulary only (`hero`, `facts`, `acts`/`ab`, `strip`, `ruler`, `donut`, `pace`, `rank`, `cards`/`card`, `chip`, `minor`, `mat`, `act`/`acthead`, `scenes`/`sc`, `qrow`/`pq`, `tags`/`tag`, `arcs`, `threads`, `soon`, `onstage`/`stage`, `moves`, `shares`, `iv`, `intermission`); none of their text goes into code, tests, or fixtures.

**Starts from:** main at `aa6a817` (v0.24.0, Drafts and Cuts, merged). Ships as **v0.24.1**.

**Execution:** subagent-driven, as for the last two plans.

## Global Constraints

- **Audience:** non-writers. None of these words appears in the page's visible text, `<title>`s, or `aria-label`s: slugline, scene heading, INT., EXT., parenthetical, extension, V.O., O.S., CONT'D, transition, action, element, beat, eighths (§5). `INT.` → Inside, `EXT.` → Outside, `INT./EXT.` → Inside and out; lengths in minutes ("2 min", "about 1 hr 50 min") and pages; places in title case, parts joined with ", ", locked numbers dropped.
- **Kind lines, verbatim:** "A screenplay in progress", "A finished screenplay", "A stage play in progress", "A finished stage play". Greeting default "What I'm writing"; explainer default "A picture of my script: its shape, its people, and every scene in order. Just scroll." The dialog's line above its button: "This page includes a few lines of dialogue and a one-line summary of each scene. Notes are never included."
- **Zero JavaScript:** no `<script>` element, no `on*` attribute, no `style` attribute, no `javascript:` URL. What varies per script is an SVG presentation attribute (`x="12.5%"`, `width`, `height`, `stroke-dasharray`, `pathLength`) or a generated class whose rule goes in the one `<style>`.
- **CSP, verbatim shape:** `default-src 'none'; style-src 'sha256-…'; font-src data:; img-src data:; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'none'` — no `script-src` at all.
- **No network:** the only URLs are `#…`, `data:`, and, when Your contact is on and the address validates (one `@`, no spaces, no `?` or `&`), one `mailto:`.
- **Escape everything the writer wrote** with `@drafter/render-html-file`'s `escapeHtml`, inside SVG `<text>` and `<title>` too. IDs and classes come from indexes (`s12`, `k3`, `a2`), never from names.
- **Dark only on screen** (`--bg #0c1315`, `--panel #131d20`, `--panel2 #18252a`, `--line #24353a`, `--fg #e7efed`, `--mute #8ba19d`, `--dim #5d7672`, `--coral #ff7b6b`, `--aqua #62d6c5`, `--sand #ecc986`, `--ember #ff9a3d`); `--dim` only for large display numbers and decoration. A light `@media print` sheet of about 1 KB (ink on white, nav hidden, cards, quotes, and rows unbroken).
- **Colours:** day = sand (DAY, MORNING, AFTERNOON), night = aqua (NIGHT), twilight = ember (DAWN, DUSK, SUNSET, SUNRISE, EVENING); LATER, SAME, CONTINUOUS, MOMENTS LATER take the scene before's time. Act steps cycle coral `#ff7b6b`, deep coral `#f0705f`, ember `#ff9a3d`. Leads, in screen-time order: `#f28fc0`, `#6fb7ff`, `#b9a2ff`, `#8fd16a`, `#ffb38a`, `#d4d97a`, `#aab8b5`; a lead who only ever speaks V.O. or O.S. is a white ring (`#ffffff`) instead; everyone else `--dim`. Colour is never the only signal.
- **Type:** Anton (display), Archivo 400 and 600 (text), Courier Prime Regular (labels, dialogue), subsetted to Latin + Latin-1 + Latin Extended-A + common punctuation, WOFF2, base64 `data:` URLs; fallbacks `"Anton", Impact, "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`, `"Archivo", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`, `"Courier Prime", "Courier New", Courier, monospace`. 390 px main layout, 16 px gutters, no sideways page scroll; title fills the width, section heads 52 px, places 22 px, quotes 24 px, body 16 px, labels 12–13 px; `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%`.
- **Budget:** under **400 KB** for a 120-page script; never over **1 MB** (300 pages, 200 scenes, 60 characters).
- **Limits:** lines of dialogue 3–18 words and ≤ 110 characters (≤ 160 when `[[Quote]]`), at most one per scene, ≥ 3 scenes apart, about one per four scenes, ≤ 12, ≤ 3 per speaker; summaries and moods ≤ 12 words; hooks ≤ 8 words; 8 cards; ranking top 12; rest list 8 + "and N more"; places top 24; arcs ≤ 12; scene lists fold into `<details>` past 80 scenes; strip blocks at least 0.15% wide.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA; one `h1` (title), `h2` per section and act, `h3` per character card, `h4` per scene; every chart `role="img"` with a `<title>`, then `<details><summary>Show as a table</summary><table>…`; decorative SVG `aria-hidden="true"`; the card row a focusable labelled region; smooth scrolling off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; axe clean at 390 and 1440 px.
- **Privacy:** never in the file: notes (except Setup/Payoff labels with Threads on), boneyard, to-do text, revision marks, the full script, any exchange, other title-page fields, contact unless on. `reopenable` is ignored: a story map never carries the source. Scene numbers are the page's own 1, 2, 3, never the export options' numbering.
- **Clean room and content:** Drafter's own layout, chart forms, and wording; no chart library; every fixture, sample, and screenshot is original text written for Drafter (§13).
- **Any regex on the writer's text is linear.** Gates with `set -e`; never `(cmd; echo $?)`; build packages before typecheck (`pnpm -r --filter "./packages/*" build`); e2e never on port 4190; never kill processes you didn't start; `pnpm install` after merges; no Co-Authored-By or any AI trailer in commits.
- **Versions named in docs:** Story map v0.24.1; Classic Mode v0.25.0; Novel v0.26.0; Production v0.27.0.

## Review Focus

1. **A cold open before the first act** (scenes before the first `#` section): they belong to Act 1 (its band block, its list, its counts), and no scene is dropped or listed twice. → Task 1 (`a cold open counts as Act 1`).
2. **Nothing written yet** (only act headings, or every scene planned): no division by zero anywhere (donuts, shares, pace, percent), the page still renders with its gaps, and "0% written" never shows without a planned length. → Task 3 (`a script of planned scenes only`), Task 6 (renders).
3. **A title page with no Title, no Author, or neither:** the title falls back to the file name, the greeting drops the initials disc and the name but keeps "What I'm writing", and "Written by" is left out of the close. → Task 4 (`no author, no disc`).
4. **Uncased scripts and names** (Japanese, Arabic, a name like `@McCLANE`): initials take the first character as it is, title-casing leaves uncased letters alone, the first/rest split falls back to the whole name, and hooks are simply empty when no capitals introduce anyone. → Task 1 (`plain words leave uncased text alone`), Task 2 (`no capitals, no hook`).
5. **Interval notes in odd places:** `[[Interval]]` in a one-act play, inside a scene, or twice; `[[No interval]]` with `[[Interval]]`. One interval at most, placed at the end of the act holding the first `[[Interval]]` (never after the last act); `[[No interval]]` anywhere wins. → Task 3 (`interval notes in odd places`).

- Length rule (Call 4): after `cleanSpeech`, 3–18 words, ≤ 110 characters (≤ 160 when marked), ends with `.`, `!`, `?`, `”`, `"`, or `’`; doesn't start with "and", "but", "so", "yeah", "well", "oh", "um", "no" (case-insensitive, as a whole word); never a dual-dialogue cue; never `[[No quote]]`.
- Marks: a `[[Quote]]` / `[[No quote]]` note belongs to the speech whose blocks (cue to the end of its last dialogue or parenthetical) hold it (`NoteEntry.block`), or the speech directly above when it is alone on the line after one.
- Spread (Call 5), in this order: every marked candidate (≤ 160 chars; the first in its scene, at most 12) is taken first; then, **as built, by even stretches**: the scenes are cut into `min(12, max(1, round(scenes / 4)))` even stretches, and each stretch without a marked line takes its best unmarked candidate by score (the review's justified deviation: scene by scene front-loaded the lines, all in the first scenes), kept when the scene is ≥ 3 from every pick, the speaker has < 3 picks, and the total is < `min(12, max(1, round(scenes / 4)))`; then one per act that has a candidate but no pick (the best in it, if it keeps the 3-apart and per-speaker rules). Score: 3 for 6–12 words, 1 for 3–5 or 13–18; +2 when it is the scene's last speech; +2 when its speaker has no pick yet (applied as picks are made); ties go to the earlier scene. Same input, same picks.
- Signature line: the character's best candidate by the same score, a `[[Quote]]` first; may equal a scene pick.
- Hooks (Call 13), as built after review: the first mention in capitals with an age, a comma, or a dash after it, else the first plain one, never a possessive; screenwriting marks are stripped from the action first. The plan's first wording: the first action paragraph where the name appears in capitals (via `@drafter/cast`'s `nameMap`/`matchNames` on that block's printed text); age from a parenthesis right after the name (`(71)`, `(40s)`, `(late 30s)`: at most 12 characters, holding a digit); the hook the rest of that sentence after the name and age, without a leading comma or dash, ≤ 8 words + "…". An edited hook in `options.hooks[key]` wins; empty string there means none.
- Threads (Call 9): `setupsAndPayoffs(noteEntries(doc))`; `from` = the scene of the first setup, `to` = the scene of the first payoff after it, null when `unpaid`. Labels only. Left out when Threads is off or there is no setup. Up to 12 drawn (Task 5 limits), all listed.
