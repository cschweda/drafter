# keep-with-next.fountain — derivation

Template `us-screenplay`; body 54 lines (0–53). A keep-with-next block (headings, cues, parentheticals) checks a chain before it is placed: itself, any keep-with-next blocks right after it, then `min(2, lines)` of the next block, plus 1 for `(MORE)` if that block would split there (§5.3). If the chain doesn't fit in what's left but fits on an empty page, a new page starts.

**Page 1.** F1 is 50 lines at 0–49; 4 lines are left (50–53).
- Heading (source 52) alone needs 2 + 1 = 3, which fits, but the chain adds 1 + 2 lines of A1: 6 > 4. It moves to **page 2**.

**Page 2.** Heading at **0**; A1 (5 lines) at **2**–6; F2 (43 lines) at **8**–50; 3 lines are left (51–53).
- MARGARET (source 104) alone needs 1 + 1 = 2, which fits, but the chain adds 2 dialogue lines and a `(MORE)`: 5 > 3. The speech also can't split, since 3 − 2 = 1 line is left for it. It moves to **page 3**.

**Page 3.** MARGARET at **0**, dialogue **1**–4; F3 (42 lines) at **6**–47; 6 lines are left (48–53).
- Heading (source 153) needs 3; the chain continues through the cue DANNY (1 + 1 = 2), then 2 dialogue lines, plus 1 for `(MORE)`, since after 3 + 2 only 1 line would remain for 4 lines of dialogue: 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 8 > 6. Heading and cue move together to **page 4**.

**Page 4.** Heading at **0**, DANNY at **2**, dialogue **3**–6.

**Pages:** 4. No page ends on a heading or a cue.
