Template `us-screenplay`: the body is 66 − 6 − 6 = **54 lines** (0–53) and the text column is 85 − 15 − 10 = **60 characters**. Space before: heading 2, action 1, cue 1, transition 1, centered 1; parentheticals and dialogue 0 inside a speech. Space before is dropped at the top of a page. Every filler line is shorter than its role's width, so each source line prints as one line.
