# Final Draft samples

`short-film.fdx` (a four-scene short, *The Spare Key*) and `stage-play.fdx` (a two-act play, *Haberdashery*) are synthetic Final Draft files for trying Drafter's import: Open either one in Drafter and it comes in as a new, unsaved Fountain script, with anything Drafter has no element for as a `[[fdx: …]]` note that Problems lists. The stage play opens as a screenplay: its acts come in as action with `[[fdx: New Act]]` and `[[fdx: End of Act]]` notes, and its scenes as forced scene headings, for you to turn into `#` and `##` sections under the Stage play template. Both were written by hand with original text and invented values, from the element names the technical design documents, without consulting any real `.fdx` file, Final Draft, or converter, and were **not** made in Final Draft, so they show the shape the design describes, not exactly what Final Draft writes. They are byte-identical copies of test files in `packages/fdx/test/fixtures/realistic/`, and the app doesn't ship them.
