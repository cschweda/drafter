# Chapter One

## The Station

The last train of the evening pulled out and left the platform to Ines. She stood with her case between her feet and watched the lights shrink to a point.

* * *

The stationmaster's window glowed. She knocked, and it opened a hand's width.

"You're early," said a voice. "Or very late."
